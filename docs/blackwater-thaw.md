# Blackwater Thaw (BT1)

An original mountain hamlet in a late-winter thaw, built around a stepped waterworks, after the user's specification
(*Blackwater Thaw: Battle Foundry map and rendering specification*). Everything is generated by
`tools/mapgen/maps/blackwater_thaw.py` (`<python> -I tools/mapgen/build.py blackwater_thaw`): the terrain and its fine
sculpt layer, the snow mask, the creek, the map's own assets (`bt_*`), the scenario, the cameras and the sound anchors.
Nothing is hand-patched in the map file.

## Layout

Spec X east / Y north = engine x / z; elevations are metres above the lower creek bed (world y 10). Playable envelope
260 x 220 m (x -128..132, z -100..120) inside a 440 x 380 m dressed floor; the terrain reaches ~390 m from the centre.

| Area | Centre | Floor |
|---|---|---|
| Lower cart yard (lower start, 10) | -87, -72 | +1 |
| Western orchard (terraces) | -76, -20 | +4 .. +9 |
| Creek bend and footbridge | 12, -31 | bed 0, crossing +2 |
| Waterworks court (centre) | -3, 2 | +4 |
| Eastern workshop yards | 66, -1 | +3.5 .. +7.5 |
| Washhouse terrace (upper start behind, 10) | 10, 56 | +15 |
| Quarry overlook | 80, 72 | +20 |
| Cable-haul station | ~236, 242 (outside the NE boundary) | ~+120 |

Routes: west (cart road over the stone bridge, orchard lane, outer trail), centre (creek path, plank footbridge at the
bend, court, north lane), east (south bank, ford, workshop lane, service stair, quarry loop); cross-links at the orchard
breach, the covered court walkway, the workshop gate, the washhouse rear yard and the terrace. Mode: Team Deathmatch,
10 v 10. First arrival at the court: lower 24.5 s, upper 23.3 s (1.2 s apart; `--sd-timings`, rules.sd.centre).

## Engine features added for it (each a per-map switch; other maps unchanged)

- `snow` (src/snow.cpp): the shared snow layer: mask (deposit x retention, traffic, melt, pooling), the spec's
  `saturate(slope_response * deposit * retention - traffic - melt)`, world noise, shelter from the sky-occlusion map;
  dry snow / slush / meltwater; caps on roofs, wall tops, rails; `snowStepAt(x, z)` for footsteps.
- `look.fogLayer {density, height}`, `look.hazeHeight`: valley mist and near air.
- `look.snowfall`: two-group, world-stable snowfall, hidden under roofs, off indoors, gusts; density by sky quality.
- water.cpp: graded stream ribbons (`path` [x, z, level]), still edges (`halfWidth`), thin ice (`ice`).
- `look.grassDead`, `look.leafless`; `vent_smoke` (lights.cpp).
- Tests: `--sd-timings` measures team-mode maps (first arrival at `rules.sd.centre`); `--blocker-scan` walks designed
  routes (`rules.sd.walkRoutes`), shoulders, entrances, gates and stairs.

## Not done / not verified

- The six building families are catalogue stand-ins at the spec's sizes, not a new kit with its own trim sheet.
- Footprints; the 45-60 s HUD-free traversal video; median / 95th / 99th percentile timings over three runs at 1440p.
- A backdrop to 2 km (the terrain ends ~390 m out); the boundary is a varied rock face, not modelled cliffs.
- Imported-model buildings (pa_ ruins) take no snow caps (the model shader also draws soldiers).
- Shared-asset stairs: upstairs in house_farmhouse_2story and vil_stone_barn cannot be walked up (flights end 0.5-0.65 m
  under the floor), on every map that uses them.
- Audio is the sound session's (anchors: content/templates/blackwater_thaw.audio.json).
