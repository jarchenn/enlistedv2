# Asset provenance

The build contains no third-party art, textures, models or audio.

- **Geometry.** Generated by `tools/build_assets.mjs` from parametric components in `tools/parts.mjs`, written for this project.
  - The forms follow common rural Norman building practice: rubble limestone walls with dressed quoins and sills, slate roofs with clay ridge tiles, ledged-and-braced plank doors, inward-opening French casements with exterior board shutters, a hearth with a timber mantel beam, an *armoire normande*, a *vaisselier* (dresser) and a long-case (Comtoise-style) clock.
  - The two staircases in the farmhouse are a gameplay adaptation. Many period farmhouses had one stair; the second, rear service stair gives every floor two independent routes.
- **Surface detail.** Procedural GLSL patterns, in `src/render.cpp`.
- **Sounds.** Synthesised at startup in `src/audio.cpp`: latch clicks, hinge creak, frame knocks, steel clang, footsteps, wind.
- **Fonts and UI.** Dear ImGui's built-in ProggyClean font.

**Not yet done: period-reference photography.** The spec asks for furniture, fittings and materials to be checked against period photographs. That review hasn't happened; the furniture is built from general knowledge of Norman rural interiors.
