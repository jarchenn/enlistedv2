## Rendering reference

BSL Shaders v10.1.8 by Capt Tatsu (https://capttatsu.com) was the visual reference for the lighting, atmosphere and post-processing pass (`docs/rendering.md`). The pack ships no licence file and no reuse terms were found, so none of its code, tables or textures are used or redistributed. The engine's shaders are original implementations, and the pack is not part of this repository.

Author, licence and source for the grenade, StG 44, M3, Kar 98k ZF39 and soldier rows come from each file's own glTF `asset.extras` (Sketchfab export fields). They were read from the files, not taken from the filenames.

## Textures: ambientCG (CC0)

The photo texture sets in `content/textures/` (L4, `docs/textures.md`) come from **[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com)** by Lennart Demes. They are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**, so no attribution is required; it is given here anyway. Only the 1K-JPG downloads are used. Their Color, NormalGL, Roughness, AmbientOcclusion and Displacement maps are repacked into `color.jpg`, `normal.jpg` and `rma.jpg`.

- The curated list of 62 asset ids (57, plus 5 for painted ground) is in `tools/texture_sets.json`.
- Each set's `meta.json` names its ambientCG asset and source URL (`https://ambientcg.com/a/<id>`).
- Fetched with `tools/fetch_ambientcg.py` through the official API.

## Grass plant atlas: Poly Haven (CC0)

The pasture grass cards (GT1, `docs/grass-atlas.md`) draw plants from two **[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)** atlases. Both are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**; attribution is given anyway.

- [grass_bermuda_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_bermuda_01): color and alpha, 2K.
- [grass_medium_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01): color, color_dry and alpha, 1K. Only its five bottom clumps and three seed plumes are used.

`tools/pack_grass_atlas.mjs` repacks them into `content/textures/grass_atlas/`: one 256 px layer per plant, dilated, with a JSON of cell rectangles and species. They are listed under `polyhaven` in `tools/texture_sets.json`.
