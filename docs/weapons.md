# Weapon reference cards

Generated by `node tools/weapon_cards.cjs` from `content/weapons/*.json` and the station blocks of `content/assets/gun_*.json`. Edit the data, not this file.

**Game numbers are tuning, not measurements.** Rates, velocities, recoil and reload times are values chosen so the sandbox feels plausible; they are not claims about a particular weapon on a particular day. Where a figure comes from the brief's sources it is cited inline in the weapon note (bracketed numbers below).

**No reference images were downloaded or shipped.** The brief lists photographs (carriers, pouches, the preserved PaK 40 optic). They were used only as descriptions to check proportions and container capacities; nothing from those pages is redistributed. All geometry is generated from boxes and cylinders by `tools/catalog_*.mjs`.

## Ammunition accounting

- Every round lives in exactly one place: the weapon, a reserve container (clip, magazine, belt segment, each with its own count), a station, or a crate. Reloads move containers; nothing is created or destroyed except by firing.
- A crate refills whole containers up to the weapon's allowance (loaded + reserve of the shipped preset). It never tops up a partial container and never exceeds the allowance.
- Finite crates count down; an empty crate refuses. Returning to Creative (F5) discards the session: crates, stations and personal loads go back to their authored values.
